Countersign logo

Two halves of an S that only make sense together. A change that moves money needs two people: the one who wrote it, and the one who signs it off. Where they meet, there's a spark.

The idea

Two halves lock together into an S, which also reads as a $: it is about money.

The two people: the developer who makes the change, and the payments lead who countersigns it. Neither half stands alone.

The orange spark is the moment of sign-off, where the two halves meet.
